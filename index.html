Anti-VEGF interval tracker

A few words that hold the dates of the last two injections for each eye, the plan for the next injections (interval and any moved dates) and the latest review date. Drugs and OCT results aren't included. The dates and results are inside the words, so nothing is stored anywhere. Write the words in the notes, then enter them here next time. A code reads correctly for 5 years.
